ENGAGEMENT BRIEF 01

One agreed scope.
Three useful deliverables.

  1. Current-state summary

    An owner interview and a basic inventory of the software, vendors, recurring costs, and workflows within the agreed scope.

  2. Prioritized recommendations

    Operational gaps and inefficient processes, with recommendations for what to address first and what can wait.

  3. 90-day improvement roadmap

    Prioritized actions for the next 90 days, with suggested ownership and dependencies to discuss with your team.

How the scope is fixed

Before work begins, we agree in writing on the business area, tools and workflows to review, participant interviews, available documentation, deliverables, timing, and fee. The review includes a kickoff conversation and a findings walkthrough.

You provide access to the agreed information and relevant people. This is an operational IT review. Implementation and ongoing support are separate engagements.
